Week 7 Preview: Corey vs. the Commish, the Shough Boyz Trade Bowl, and the Dill's QB is on bye

Week 7 Preview

Four NFL teams are on bye this week: Buffalo, Jacksonville, the Chargers and Washington. Projections and lineups are Yahoo's as of Tuesday afternoon (about 1:10pm ET), and every lineup can still change before kickoff. Bye-week starters are projected for nothing, so several of these numbers go up the moment someone opens the app.

Parker? I hardly know her (proj 95.81) vs Shough Boyz (proj 88.24)

The site owner against the commissioner, and the closest projected game of the week: 7.57. Corey is 2-4 and coming off the week's top score. ryan is 0-6, just posted the season's lowest score (70.42) and traded away Justin Jefferson and Nico Collins. The roster he has left: Dak Prescott, Brian Robinson Jr., Matthew Golden, Colston Loveland and Josh Jacobs, with new arrival Brian Thomas Jr. (JAX) on the bench, on bye. Corey's problem is the namesake: Parker Washington (JAX) is in a starting WR slot on bye, with Ja'Kobi Lane on the bench. Parker? You hardly need him this week.

Pete (proj 107.51) vs 1 and 13 (proj 124.58)

The Shough Boyz Trade Bowl. Both of these teams got a WR1 from the commish in the last two days, and both of them are starting him: Nico Collins for Pete and Justin Jefferson for 1 and 13. Matthew has the top projection of the week. david has the bigger to-do list: his DEF slot is empty after dropping the Packers on Tuesday morning. Yahoo has 1 and 13 by 17.07. One of these teams is going to beat the other with ryan's receiver, and ryan will be watching.

Radio (proj 89.78) vs Waz (proj 109.60)

Josh Allen is in the Dill's QB slot, and Buffalo is on bye. There's no other quarterback on the Radio roster. The bench is Jeremiyah Love (Q), Blake Corum, Cooper Kupp and three injured guys. The Dill needs a waiver QB before Sunday or he's playing a man down against the league's top scorer. andrew has won five of six and is favored by 19.82. The Dill has made one move all season, so this is his chance to make it two.

Witness da thickness (proj 109.78) vs Hail King Gunther (proj 86.95)

The two hottest teams in the league over the last two weeks (Gunther 125.58, Witness 124.23), and both on two-game win streaks. stephen has a bye problem: James Cook III (BUF) and Terry McLaurin (WAS, listed D) are both in his starting lineup, with Trevor Lawrence (JAX) also on bye on the bench. That's why he's projected for only 86.95. Aaron is starting Mahomes with Jordan Love on the bench, plus two defenses again, the Broncos starting and the Chiefs in reserve. Witness by 22.83 on paper. Fix the lineup and Gunther gets a lot closer.

Hard On (proj 114.94) vs Big Fudge (proj 79.32)

The biggest projected gap of the week: 35.62. Max's lineup has Jayden Daniels (WAS) at QB and Rachaad White (WAS) at flex, both on bye, and his only other quarterback is Jalen Hurts, who's on IR. Ladd McConkey (LAC) and Brenton Strange (JAX) are on the bench on bye too. Rob has his own Week 6 top scorer, Bhayshul Tuten (JAX), on the bench for the bye, with Kyler Murray starting at QB over Kirk Cousins. Big Fudge has lost five straight, and right now it doesn't have a quarterback.

Desk Picks

Last week the desk went 4-1. The miss was picking against Corey, and he hasn't let us forget it. This week: Parker? I hardly know her (we learned), 1 and 13, Waz, Witness da thickness and Hard On. If the Dill finds a quarterback by Sunday, we reserve the right to be nervous about that one.
