Week 10 Preview: Yahoo Picks the Commish, Waz Needs a Defense Again, and Rob Needs a Quarterback, a Flex and a Defense

Week 10 Preview

Four NFL teams are on bye this week: Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia and Tampa Bay. That's a lot of bye weeks, and it shows in the lineups. Projections and lineups are Yahoo's as of Tuesday afternoon (about 1:15pm ET), and every lineup can still change. Empty slots and bye-week starters are projected for zero, so several of these numbers should go up once managers check the app. Also: the NFL trade deadline was today, but the league's fantasy trade deadline is Saturday, Nov 28, so there is still time to fleece someone.

Parker? I hardly know her (proj 95.09) vs Waz (proj 107.19)

The site owner gets the top team in the league, with the best form in the league (139.32), and Yahoo has Corey down 12.10. That gap would be bigger, except Waz's DEF slot is empty. The Bears D andrew picked up last week to cover the Steelers' bye is now on its own bye, so it's on the bench, and he doesn't have another defense. Last week he fixed this by Friday. D'Andre Swift, Caleb Williams and Dallas Goedert are also on bye and already benched, and A.J. Brown (Q) is on the bench. Corey has his own bye problem: Jaylen Waddle (Q, Denver) is starting at W/T. His other Denver player, J.K. Dobbins, is on the bench, and so is Baker Mayfield (Tampa Bay). The bench options for the W/T are Emanuel Wilson, David Montgomery (Q) and Tyreek Hill (NA). The good news: Michael Penix Jr. is the starting QB again, and Lamar is in the IR slot where he belongs. Corey needs a lineup fix and a lot of help. andrew needs a defense. One of those is easier.

Josh Jacobs (O) is benched, and Kyle Monangai is in the IR slot. ryan has beaten his projection three straight weeks. If he does it again, and Max doesn't fix his RBs, it might finally be the week. We're rooting for you, ryan.
