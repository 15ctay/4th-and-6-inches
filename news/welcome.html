Welcome to 4th and '6 inches'

Ten teams. Half PPR. Rolling waivers, keepers and a trade-veto vote. Welcome to the official unofficial home of 4th and '6 inches', 2026 season. Sometimes six inches is all you need.

Here's the field:

  • Standings: W-L-T, points for and against, streaks, and the playoff line (top 6 make it, Weeks 15–17, with reseeding).
  • Results: every week, every score, Yahoo's original projections, plus the top score and biggest blowout of each week.
  • Power Rankings: our formula blends record, points for and recent form. Don't shoot the messenger.
  • Teams: a page for every squad, with the full roster by slot (including injury tags) and a season game log.
  • Keeper Tracker (new): every team's full draft, this year's keepers marked, whether each pick is still on the roster (and where he went if not), plus post-draft pickups that could be keeper candidates. Keeper rules are set by the commish.
  • News: recaps, previews and trash talk.
  • Transactions: every add, drop and trade, with search, sorting and a team filter.
  • League Info: half PPR, 4-pt pass TDs, rolling waivers (no FAB), keepers, and a trade deadline of November 28, 2026.

Every number comes straight from Yahoo Fantasy. Now go fix your lineup. Some of you really need to.
