Week 11 Preview: Six Teams on Bye, Waz Has No Tight End, Gunther Starts His Bye-Week QB, and the Site Owner Gets the First-Place Team

Week 11 Preview

Six NFL teams are on bye this week: Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, the Rams, New England and Seattle. That's the most of the season, and it hit almost everybody. Projections and lineups are Yahoo's as of Tuesday afternoon (about 1:15pm ET), and every lineup can still change. Bye-week starters and empty slots are projected for zero, so several of these numbers should go up once managers check the app. The fantasy trade deadline is Saturday, Nov 28.

Parker? I hardly know her (proj 75.71) vs Pete (proj 117.16)

Last week the site owner beat the team tied for first. This week he gets the team that's alone in first, and Yahoo has him down 41.45, the biggest gap of the week. Part of that is on Corey: Kyle Pitts Sr. (Atlanta) is starting at TE and DeMario Douglas (New England) is starting at W/T, and both are on bye. Woody Marks is starting at W/R/T with a D tag. The bench has David Montgomery, J.K. Dobbins, Audric Estime and Jaylen Waddle (Q), so the flex spots can be fixed today. The TE can't: Pitts is the only one on the roster, so that's a pickup. david has one bye of his own, Christian Watson (Green Bay) at WR, and Jalen Coker, Adonai Mitchell, Rico Dowdle and Devin Singletary on the bench to replace him. The teams met in Week 2 and Pete won 112.88-102.50. Corey needs three lineup fixes and another McCaffrey game. Pete needs to swap out one guy.

Shough Boyz (proj 74.92) vs Waz (proj 111.94)

The commish follows up his first win with the 8-2 team that leads the league in points. ryan has two bye-week starters: Jadarian Price (Seattle) at RB and Matthew Golden (Green Bay) at WR. Colston Loveland is back from his bye and on the bench, and he can play the W/T. Kaelon Black is the other healthy option. Josh Jacobs (O) and Kaleb Johnson are both Green Bay, so they're out too. Waz has the strangest lineup in the league: the TE slot is empty. andrew dropped Dallas Goedert last week for a defense, and the only TE left, Tucker Kraft, is on bye. Kenneth Walker III and Omar Cooper Jr. (0.00 last week) are both starting with Q tags. A.J. Brown (Q) and Quinshon Judkins are on bye anyway. andrew dropped Jared Goff and the Commanders D early this morning, so there's room on the roster. He only needs one tight end. Waz won the Week 2 meeting 109.72-89.46.

Radio (proj 89.81) vs Hail King Gunther (proj 60.20)

The Dill's problem is the Rams D: it's starting, it's on bye, and it's the only defense on the roster. His bench is mostly bye-week players too: Bijan Robinson, Blake Corum, Rhamondre Stevenson and Cooper Kupp. The starting nine is otherwise healthy, so one defense pickup fixes it. Dill, this would be move number five. stephen's lineup is worse. Drake Maye (D, and New England is on bye), Kyren Williams (Rams) and KC Concepcion Jr. (Cleveland) are all starting, and all on bye. Trevor Lawrence is on the bench for the QB spot, and Courtland Sutton and Chris Godwin Jr. are there for the other two. Fix those three and the 29.61 gap closes fast. Radio won the Week 2 meeting 131.92-116.82. Both teams are on four-game losing streaks, so one of them is getting off the schneid Sunday.
