Week 6 Final: Corey Posts the Week's High, Waz Ends Pete's Perfect Season, and the Commish Hits 0-6

Week 6 Final Recap

Week 6 was a defensive struggle for everybody except the people writing this site. League average: 97.20, the lowest of the season (Week 5 was 118.03). Nine of ten teams finished under their Yahoo projection. The one that didn't is Corey's. The desk would like to say that's a coincidence. It's not a coincidence, it's a 13.92-point beat.

The Games

Waz 114.40, Pete 81.54 The perfect season is over. david came in 5-0, was projected for 109.16, and finished at 81.54, 27.62 under, the worst miss of the week. Cam Skattebo (0.00) and MarShawn Lloyd (1.0) were in the lineup, and Bryce Young (19.84) was his top scorer. andrew got Kenneth Walker III (26.9), D'Andre Swift (21.0) and Caleb Williams (16.5) and won by 32.86, the biggest margin of the week, even with A.J. Brown at 4.2. Waz and Pete are both 5-1 now, and Waz takes 1st on points (746.26 to 694.24). Last week we said somebody's streak was about to move. It was david's.

Parker? I hardly know her 124.20, 1 and 13 95.88 Top score of the week, from the site owner, in the game the desk "barely" picked against him. We heard about it. The namesake, Parker Washington, only gave 8.4 this time and Jaylen Waddle gave 4.4, and it didn't matter. That's the same Michael Wilson Corey traded to Matthew for Tyreek Hill on Oct 11, so Corey won the trade on Sunday too, at least until Tyreek gets off the NA list. 1 and 13's three-game win streak ends, and Corey's 0.92-point "coin flip" ends in a 28.32-point win. ctay is 2-4.

Witness da thickness 117.88, Hard On 95.22 Good news for Rob first: the QB slot got filled. Kirk Cousins, added Saturday, scored 19.52, and Bhayshul Tuten (22.7) and Carnell Tate (16.0) helped. Hail King Gunther 90.10, Shough Boyz 70.42 A game where neither offense really showed up. Commissioner ryan's 70.42 is the lowest score in the league this season. Matthew Golden (19.4), Colston Loveland (15.5) and Dak Prescott (13.02) led the team, while Rashee Rice (0.00) and the Texans D (1.0) did not. stephen won with the lowest winning score of the week. Desk note, from the commissioner's transaction log: Then, in a span of about 24 hours (Monday 12:55am to Tuesday 1:19am), ryan traded Justin Jefferson, his first-round pick (#8 overall), to 1 and 13 for Brian Thomas Jr., and then Nico Collins, one of his two keepers, to Pete. Both of them start for their new teams in Week 7. Brian Thomas Jr. is on bye in Week 7. The commish is building for the future. We respect the process. We just can't see it from here.

Radio 96.60, Big Fudge 85.80 The closest game of the week, 10.80, and both teams missed their projections by more than 17 (Radio −17.48, Big Fudge −17.33).
